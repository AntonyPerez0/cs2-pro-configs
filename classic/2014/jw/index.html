These are JW's CS:GO settings from 2014, the year of the ranking (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 768p, JW's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922); for Static Cross at 1080p and 960p it agrees with the rule crosshairrestore.com measured from in-game screenshots (CS2 builds before Sept 30), and nothing else has been checked against captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec jw-2014 in the console.

    A config.cfg written by JW's game, from setup.gg's archive. Its Operation Breakout reminder stores a timestamp of 9 Aug 2014, and it lacks snd_menumusic_volume and the other music-volume settings that came with the 10 Oct 2014 update, so it was saved between those dates.

    How the date is known

    A config.cfg written by JW's game, from setup.gg's archive. Its Operation Breakout reminder stores a timestamp of 9 Aug 2014, and it lacks snd_menumusic_volume and the other music-volume settings that came with the 10 Oct 2014 update, so it was saved between those dates.

  • DPI (same sensitivity) · 2019-07-30 · confidence medium · github.com
    How the date is known

    From a RushB (rushb.net) article on JW's settings dated 30 Jul 2019, kept in a GitHub mirror. It gives 400 DPI at 1000 Hz with sensitivity 2.2, the same sensitivity as in the 2014 config.

  • Resolution · 2016-12-13 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    From setup.gg's profile for JW (data.yml), published on 13 Dec 2016. It still lists him at fnatic, which he left on 15 Aug 2016 (Liquipedia), so the values may be older.
