These are Snax's CS:GO settings from 2013, the closest to 2014 we found that can be converted (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 600p, Snax's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

Full config 3 parts

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec snax-2014 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair, viewmodel, HUD and radar · 2013 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Game-written config.cfg from setup.gg's archive. The only timestamp in it is from 13 Jan 2013, and its game-written lines lack the radar settings added on 25 Apr 2013 (the unquoted cl_radar_scale line at the end was added by hand), so it was saved between those dates. setup.gg later edited the gap from -2 to 0 (1 May 2016); the original -2 is used.

  • Sensitivity · 2013 · confidence medium · github.com
    How the date is known

    From the same game-written config.cfg as the crosshair: the only timestamp in it is from 13 Jan 2013 and its game-written lines lack the radar settings added on 25 Apr 2013, so it was saved between those dates.

  • DPI (same sensitivity) · 2016-12-13 · confidence low · github.com
    How the date is known

    setup.gg's profile for Snax (data.yml), added to its data repository on 13 Dec 2016 and never changed. It lists the same sensitivity as the 2013 config, 1.9, at 400 DPI; when the values were collected isn't stated.

  • Resolution · 2016-12-13 · confidence low · github.com
    How the date is known

    From setup.gg's profile for Snax (data.yml), added to its data repository on 13 Dec 2016 and never changed. It doesn't say when the settings were collected.

DPI is only taken from a source that lists the same sensitivity, so the eDPI matches the sensitivity shown. The other parts can come from configs of different dates, as listed.
