These are RpK's CS:GO settings from 2016-05-27, the closest to 2012 we found that can be converted (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 768p, RpK's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922); for Static Cross at 1080p and 960p it agrees with the rule crosshairrestore.com measured from in-game screenshots (CS2 builds before Sept 30), and nothing else has been checked against captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair · 2016-05-27 · confidence low · liquipedia.net 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Liquipedia's RpK page first lists a Crosshair table in revision 370048 of 27 May 2016, when he played for G2, and kept it until 29 Jun 2016 (revision 387359). The table cites no source, so the date is when it was added to the page, not when he set it. That revision lists 1024x768 stretched, the resolution shown here. Its outline thickness 0 is read as the outline off, and cl_crosshairusealpha 1 is assumed for its alpha 200, as the table gives no usealpha.

  • Sensitivity · 2016 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    setup.gg's player file for RpK, added on 13 Dec 2016 with his team given as G2 (joined Feb 2016), lists sensitivity 2 at 400 DPI and 500 Hz. The file has no date of its own, so 13 Dec 2016 is only the latest possible date.

  • Resolution · 2016 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    The same setup.gg player file, added on 13 Dec 2016 with no date of its own, lists 1024x768 at 4:3. The May 2016 Liquipedia revision the crosshair comes from lists 1024x768 too, stretched.

DPI is only taken from a source that lists the same sensitivity, so the eDPI matches the sensitivity shown. The other parts can come from configs of different dates, as listed.
