These are RpK's CS:GO settings from 2021-01-05, the closest to 2012 we found that can be converted (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 1024p, the resolution the crosshair's source lists; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec rpk-2012 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair · 2021-01-05 · confidence medium · github.com, liquipedia.net, params.gg
    How the date is known

    Liquipedia's RpK page, read from a copy cached in Oct 2023, marks its crosshair table 'Updated as of 2021-01-05' and cites params.gg. Its hardware table, with the same date, lists 1280x1024 stretched, so the crosshair is converted at 1024p.

  • Sensitivity · 2016 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    setup.gg's player file for RpK, added on 13 Dec 2016 with his team given as G2 (joined Feb 2016), lists sensitivity 2 at 400 DPI and 500 Hz. The file has no date of its own, so 13 Dec 2016 is only the latest possible date.

  • Resolution · 2016 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    The same setup.gg player file, added on 13 Dec 2016 with no date of its own, lists 1024x768 at 4:3. This is the resolution shown here; the crosshair is converted at the 1280x1024 of its own 2021 source.

DPI is only taken from a source that lists the same sensitivity, so the eDPI matches the sensitivity shown. The other parts can come from configs of different dates, as listed.
