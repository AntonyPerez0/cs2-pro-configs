These are SmithZz's CS:GO settings from 2013, the closest to 2012 we found that can be converted (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 768p, SmithZz's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

Full config 3 parts

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec smithzz-2012 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair, viewmodel and HUD · 2013 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    His VeryGames autoexec.cfg, published by setup.gg in 2016, is headed 'VERYGAMES SmithZz - 04/01/2013': 4 Jan or 1 Apr 2013, depending on the day/month order. Nothing in it needs a later game version, and he played for VeryGames until the end of 2013.

  • Sensitivity · 2013 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Same autoexec.cfg as the crosshair, headed 'VERYGAMES SmithZz - 04/01/2013' (4 Jan or 1 Apr 2013, depending on the day/month order).

  • DPI (same sensitivity) · 2016 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    setup.gg's player file for SmithZz, added on 13 Dec 2016 with no date of its own, lists 400 DPI and 1000 Hz with the same 1.88 sensitivity. prosettings.net still listed 400 DPI at 1.88 in May 2024.

  • Resolution · 2016 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    The same setup.gg player file, added on 13 Dec 2016 with no date of its own, lists 1024x768 at 4:3. prosettings.net listed the same resolution in May 2024.

  • Photo · G2 Esports video (YouTube) · 2018-03-07 · by G2 Esports · CC BY 3.0 · via Wikimedia Commons · cropped

DPI is only taken from a source that lists the same sensitivity, so the eDPI matches the sensitivity shown. The other parts can come from configs of different dates, as listed.
