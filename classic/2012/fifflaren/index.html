  • Style 2 was the original classic crosshair until February 2014: it showed when you fired but not weapon accuracy (style 5 later recreated it). In CS2, style 2 is the later, accurate version, so it spreads more with movement and inaccuracy than this crosshair did.
  • Not in this config because the setting didn't exist yet, so off: cl_crosshair_drawoutline, cl_crosshairgap_useweaponvalue, cl_crosshair_t, cl_crosshair_recoil.
  • Rounding and dropping the fraction give your old size or thickness different whole pixels; the converter rounds, as other converters and community measurements do, so the old game may have drawn it 1 px smaller.
  • Odd-width bars sit on a different centre pixel in the new game, so the crosshair may appear 1 px off.
  • Exported style 2 keeps the old style family. Its at-rest shape is assumed to match Static Cross, but it moves with inaccuracy and shots, which is neither modelled nor checked in game.

These are Fifflaren's CS:GO settings from 2012-12-13, the year of the ranking (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 1080p (their resolution isn't listed); CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec fifflaren-2012 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair · 2012-12-13 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    Style and values from the 'NiP Fifflaren' preset in Skarbo's CS:GO crosshair generator, already in its first commit on 13 Dec 2012. His own late-2012 NiP config.cfg has the same colour, size, thickness, gap and dot, but no style setting.

  • Sensitivity · 2012 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Game-written config.cfg saved under the name 'NiP-Fifflaren-', published by setup.gg in 2016. It has cl_crosshairgap (added 22 Aug 2012) but no cl_crosshairstyle, which Skarbo's generator already used on 13 Dec 2012, so it was most likely saved between those dates.

  • DPI (assumed) · 2016-12-13 · github.com
    Why it is assumed

    No source lists a DPI with this sensitivity, so the DPI matcher assumes the 400 DPI of their setup.gg profile, which lists sensitivity 2.2; change it on the page if you know better.
