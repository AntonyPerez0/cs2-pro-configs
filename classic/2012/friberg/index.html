Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair · 2012-12-13 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Upper bound only. The 'nip_friberg' preset is already in the first commit (e569127, 2012-12-13 16:12 +0100) of Skarbo/CSGOCrosshair, so it was written on or before that day. When friberg actually used it is unknown.

  • Sensitivity · 2012-08-11 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    The date is self-reported, and only the autoexec carries it. autoexec.cfg ends with `echo "NiP-friberg- CS:GO config loaded"` and `echo "Updated: 2012-08-11"`. config.cfg has no date of its own. Both files were first committed to setupgg/csgo in a7612a6 on 2016-04-29, with no source given. config.cfg can only be placed in a range. Its name tag is "NiP-friberg-", and the cached Liquipedia history dates his NiP tenure from 2012-08-10. It has cl_dynamiccrosshair and no cl_crosshairstyle or cl_crosshairgap, while Skarbo's generator already lists those two cvars on 2012-12-13. That fits roughly Aug–Dec 2012, but it cannot pin config.cfg to 2012-08-11. The two files are also not one snapshot. config.cfg does not reflect several of the autoexec's settings: cl_autohelp 1 vs 0, cl_detail_max_sway 5 vs 0, cl_detail_avoid_force 0.4 vs 0, func_break_max_pieces 15 vs 0, bind KP_INS volume 0.5 vs 0.75, and cl_crosshairsize 2 vs the autoexec's final 1500.

  • Resolution · 2016-12-13 · confidence medium · github.com
    How the date is known

    Upper bound only. This is the date of the initial commit (afdec47, 2016-12-13 17:37 +0200) of setupgg/data, which added player/friberg/data.yml. The file is unchanged since. setupgg gives no source or collection date. The file's 'team: nip' fits the cached Liquipedia NiP tenure (2012-08-10 – 2017-07-31).

Settings from different sources are only combined when they agree (DPI is only paired with a sensitivity from the same source), so the eDPI never mixes eras.
