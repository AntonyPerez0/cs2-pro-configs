Classic configs. 2011–2015

The top 10 players of each year from 2011–2015, plus Cloud9 and iBUYPOWER in January 2015, with their CS:GO settings converted for CS2: the crosshair converted to the new crosshair system, sensitivity, viewmodel and the rest as console commands. Each config is dated and sourced; when nothing from the exact time is documented (or it can't be converted), the closest one is used and its date shown.

2011 2012 2013 2014 2015 C9 & iBP · Jan 2015

Rankings: HLTV's Top 20 Players of the Year (2011, 2013-2015) and Esports Earnings' 2012 CS:GO prize money. Configs come from public archives of pro CS:GO configs (mostly game-written config.cfg files in setup.gg's GitHub archive) and Liquipedia's settings tables. A date comes from the settings a file contains (checked against Valve's release notes), from a date written in the file or on its source (a header comment, Liquipedia's "Updated as of"), or else from the archive's commit date as an upper bound; each page lists its sources and how each date is known.

Cloud9 and iBUYPOWER

January 2015 gosugamers.net 1, 2, cybersport.ru

North America's two leading teams at the start of 2015. On 15 December 2014 Cloud9 swapped Hiko for ShahZaM and Hiko joined iBUYPOWER, which had just brought back DaZeD. On 5 January the iBUYPOWER five left the organisation and played on as GX until late January, when Valve banned DaZeD, swag and AZK, along with former teammate steel, over a match the 2014 iBUYPOWER team had thrown. steel, who had left in November 2014, is listed with them.
