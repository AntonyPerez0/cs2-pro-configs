No convertible crosshair of his from closer to this lineup could be found: this one is ProSettings.net's listing of his Counter-Strike 2 settings, last updated in January 2025, ten years after this lineup, and may differ from what he used then. It is a third-party table, not his own config. It gives an alpha value of 1, which would make the crosshair almost invisible, so that value is left out and the default opacity is used. Two fan apps from 2017 list the same sensitivity, 1.4, at 800 DPI instead of 400.

  • Rounding and dropping the fraction give your old size or thickness different whole pixels; the converter rounds, as other converters and community measurements do, so the old game may have drawn it 1 px smaller.

These are Skadoodle's pre-update CS2 settings from 2025-01-28, the closest to January 2015 we found that can be converted (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 768p, Skadoodle's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec skadoodle-c9-ibp-2015 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair and viewmodel · 2025-01-28 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2, prosettings.net
    How the date is known

    ProSettings.net's Counter-Strike 2 settings for him, from a copy of the page saved to GitHub on 3 Nov 2025 and marked last updated 28 Jan 2025. A scrape of the page uploaded to GitHub on 10 Jan 2024 already listed the same length, thickness, gap and colour but gave the style as Unknown.

  • Sensitivity · 2016 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    setup.gg's profile for him (data.yml), first published on 13 Dec 2016, lists sensitivity 1.4 at 400 DPI with raw input on and no acceleration. It lists him on Cloud9, which he joined on 29 Apr 2015 (Liquipedia), so the values most likely date from between then and 13 Dec 2016.

  • Resolution · 2016 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    The same setup.gg profile lists 1024x768 at 4:3. It has no date of its own; it lists him on Cloud9, which he joined on 29 Apr 2015, and was published on 13 Dec 2016.
