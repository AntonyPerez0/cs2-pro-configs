These are shroud's CS:GO settings from 2014-07, the closest to January 2015 we found that can be converted (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 1080p, shroud's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec shroud-c9-ibp-2015 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair, viewmodel, HUD and radar · 2014-07 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    shroud's game-written config.cfg (in-game name 'shroud') from setup.gg's archive. Its Operation Breakout reminder stores a timestamp of 5 Jul 2014, and it lacks cl_radar_square_with_scoreboard and engine_no_focus_sleep, both added on 23 Jul 2014, so it was saved between those dates.

  • Sensitivity · 2014-07 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Same game-written config.cfg as the crosshair (sensitivity 2.1375, zoom ratio 1, raw input off), saved between 5 Jul 2014 (its Operation Breakout reminder timestamp) and 23 Jul 2014 (settings it lacks).

  • Resolution · 2016-12-13 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    shroud's setup.gg profile (data.yml), added on 13 Dec 2016 with his team as Cloud9 and unchanged since, lists 1920x1080 at 16:9; that is the latest date it can be from. Its sensitivity (2 at 400 DPI) differs from the 2014 config's 2.1375, so its DPI is not used.

  • DPI (assumed) · 2016-12-13 · github.com
    Why it is assumed

    No source lists a DPI with this sensitivity, so the DPI matcher assumes the 400 DPI of their setup.gg profile, which lists sensitivity 2; change it on the page if you know better.
