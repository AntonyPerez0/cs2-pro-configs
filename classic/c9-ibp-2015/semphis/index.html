  • cl_crosshairalpha 999999 is outside 0-255; the old game drew it as 255.
  • Rounding and dropping the fraction give your old size or thickness different whole pixels; the converter rounds, as other converters and community measurements do, so the old game may have drawn it 1 px smaller.
  • Odd-width bars sit on a different centre pixel in the new game, so the crosshair may appear 1 px off.
  • The full outline (cl_crosshair_drawoutline 1) is modelled as 1 px all round; no game capture checks it yet.

These are Semphis's CS:GO settings from 2013, the closest to January 2015 we found that can be converted (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 900p, Semphis's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair, viewmodel, HUD and radar · 2013 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Semphis's game-written config.cfg (in-game name 'coL.SoundBlaster | SEMPHIS') from setup.gg's archive. Its medal-stats timestamp is 28 Aug 2013, and it lacks lobby_voice_chat_enabled, which matches the 23 Jan 2014 change that made the lobby microphone button remember its state, so it was saved between those dates.

  • Sensitivity · 2013 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Same game-written config.cfg as the crosshair (sensitivity 1.7, zoom ratio 1), saved between 28 Aug 2013 (its medal-stats timestamp) and 23 Jan 2014 (lobby_voice_chat_enabled missing).

  • Resolution · 2016-12-13 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Semphis's setup.gg profile (data.yml), added on 13 Dec 2016 with his team as TSM and unchanged since, lists 1600x900; that is the latest date it can be from. Its aspect-ratio field says 4:3, which doesn't fit 1600x900, so only the resolution is shown, and its sensitivity (1.5 at 450 DPI) differs from the config's 1.7, so its DPI is not used.

DPI is only taken from a source that lists the same sensitivity, so the eDPI matches the sensitivity shown. The other parts can come from configs of different dates, as listed.
