These are seang@res's CS:GO settings from 2019-04-09, the closest to January 2015 we found that can be converted (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 960p, the resolution the crosshair's source lists; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922); for Static Cross at 1080p and 960p it agrees with the rule crosshairrestore.com measured from in-game screenshots (CS2 builds before Sept 30), and nothing else has been checked against captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec seangares-c9-ibp-2015 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair · 2019-04-09 · confidence low · github.com, liquipedia.net, clips.twitch.tv
    How the date is known

    Liquipedia's seang@res page (revision 2624925, cached on 21 Oct 2023 in Lindeneg/csgo-pro-settings) marks its crosshair table 'Updated as of 2019-04-09' and cites a Twitch clip. Its hardware table (updated 31 Oct 2020) lists 1280x960 stretched, so the crosshair is converted at 960p.

  • Sensitivity · 2016-12-13 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    setup.gg's profile for sgares (data.yml), added on 13 Dec 2016 and unchanged since, lists sensitivity 1.9 at 400 DPI, raw input off and no acceleration. It has no date of its own, so 13 Dec 2016 is only the latest date these can be from.

  • Resolution · 2016-12-13 · confidence low · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Same setup.gg profile (data.yml), added on 13 Dec 2016 and unchanged since, lists 1920x1080 at 16:9. 13 Dec 2016 is only the latest date this can be from.
