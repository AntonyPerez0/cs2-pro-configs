These are device's CS:GO settings from 2014, the closest to 2015 we found that can be converted (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 960p, device's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922); for Static Cross at 1080p and 960p it agrees with the rule crosshairrestore.com measured from in-game screenshots (CS2 builds before Sept 30), and nothing else has been checked against captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec device-2015 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair, viewmodel, HUD and radar · 2014 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    device's config.cfg (name "device )))") from setup.gg's archive. Its game-written part has spec_usenumberkeys_nobinds (added 27 Feb 2014) but not cl_scoreboard_mouse_enable_binding (added 23 Apr 2014), so the game saved it between those dates. Some lines were added by hand later.

  • Sensitivity · 2014 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Same config.cfg as the crosshair (sensitivity 2.2, in its game-written part), saved by the game between 27 Feb 2014 (spec_usenumberkeys_nobinds present) and 23 Apr 2014 (cl_scoreboard_mouse_enable_binding missing).

  • DPI (same sensitivity) · 2016-12-13 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    device's setup.gg profile (data.yml), added on 13 Dec 2016 and unchanged since, lists 400 DPI and 500 Hz with sensitivity 2.2, the same as the config; that is the latest date they can be from. Its team field is astralis, which Liquipedia lists him with from 18 Jan 2016.

  • Resolution · 2016-12-13 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    Same setup.gg profile (data.yml), added on 13 Dec 2016 and unchanged since, lists 1280x960 at 4:3; that is the latest date it can be from. Its team field is astralis, which Liquipedia lists him with from 18 Jan 2016.
