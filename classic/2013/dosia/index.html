These are Dosia's CS:GO settings from 2013, the year of the ranking (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 768p, Dosia's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922); for Static Cross at 1080p and 960p it agrees with the rule crosshairrestore.com measured from in-game screenshots (CS2 builds before Sept 30), and nothing else has been checked against captures.

The paste sets every setting that shapes this crosshair, including the iron-sight dot (CS2's defaults where their config has no value), so nothing from your current crosshair carries over.

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec dosia-2013 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair, viewmodel, HUD and radar · 2013 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    Game-written config.cfg from setup.gg's archive, with the in-game name 'Dosia[v] * BenQ'. It has cl_dm_buyrandomweapons and mm_dedicated_search_maxping, which came with the 23 Jan 2013 update, but not cl_radar_scale (added 25 Apr 2013), so it was saved between those dates.

  • Sensitivity · 2013 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    Game-written config.cfg from setup.gg's archive, with the in-game name 'Dosia[v] * BenQ'. It has cl_dm_buyrandomweapons and mm_dedicated_search_maxping, which came with the 23 Jan 2013 update, but not cl_radar_scale (added 25 Apr 2013), so it was saved between those dates.

  • Resolution · 2016-12-13 · confidence low · github.com, liquipedia.net 1, 2, 3
    How the date is known

    setup.gg's profile for Dosia, published in setupgg/data on 13 Dec 2016 and not changed afterwards. It does not say when the settings were collected, and its sensitivity (2.5) differs from the config's 2, so its DPI is not used. No source gives his resolution for 2013: Liquipedia's Dosia page, which starts in February 2014, first gives one in revision 145986 of 9 Jun 2015, 1600x900, which it still lists in revision 270775 of 8 Jan 2016; in revision 321581 of 13 Mar 2016 and later it lists 1024x768 with black bars, as setup.gg does. The converted crosshair may therefore be a pixel off from what he saw.

  • DPI (assumed) · 2016-12-13 · github.com
    Why it is assumed

    No source lists a DPI with this sensitivity, so the DPI matcher assumes the 400 DPI of their setup.gg profile, which lists sensitivity 2.5; change it on the page if you know better.
