These are f0rest's CS:GO settings from 2013-12-06, the year of the ranking (see where these settings come from). Converted to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 900p, f0rest's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

Full config 2 parts

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec f0rest-2013 in the console.

Where these settings come from

  • Crosshair · 2013-12-06 · confidence medium · gist.github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    A 'NiP f0rest' block in a third-party autoexec.cfg gist, first added in its 6 Dec 2013 revision. Apart from cl_crosshairstyle 4, its size, thickness, gap and colour match his own 2012 config (used for the sensitivity).

  • Sensitivity · 2012 · confidence medium · github.com 1, 2
    How the date is known

    Game-written config.cfg filed under f0rest in setup.gg's archive (in-game name 'NiP-iZnoGouD-'; its crosshair is the 'NiP f0rest' one). It has cl_crosshairgap, added 22 Aug 2012, but not cl_crosshairstyle, which the game had by 13 Dec 2012, so it was saved between those dates.

  • Resolution · 2016-12-13 · confidence medium · github.com
    How the date is known

    setup.gg's player profile (1600x900, 16:9), created in setupgg/data's first commit on 13 Dec 2016 and never changed. It gives no date or source, so this is only when it was published.

  • Photo · WCG 2011 Grand Final · 2011-12-11 · by xReDeYe · CC BY 2.0 · via Wikimedia Commons · cropped

DPI is only taken from a source that lists the same sensitivity, so the eDPI matches the sensitivity shown. The other parts can come from configs of different dates, as listed.
