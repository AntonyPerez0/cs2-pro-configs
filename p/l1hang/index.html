  • Rounding and dropping the fraction give your old size or thickness different whole pixels; the converter rounds, as other converters and community measurements do, so the old game may have drawn it 1 px smaller.
  • Odd-width bars sit on a different centre pixel in the new game, so the crosshair may appear 1 px off.

Converted from the pre-patch crosshair settings.gg lists for L1haNg to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 960p, L1haNg's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution. Many pros rebuilt their crosshair after the update, so this may not be what L1haNg uses now — see their crosshair history.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line longer than 510 characters, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec l1hang in the console.
