Converted from the pre-patch crosshair settings.gg lists for prosus to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 960p, prosus's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution. Many pros rebuilt their crosshair after the update, so this may not be what prosus uses now — see their crosshair history.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.
