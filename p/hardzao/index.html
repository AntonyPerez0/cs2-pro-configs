Converted from the pre-patch crosshair settings.gg lists for hardzao to CS2's crosshair system since the Sept 22, 2026 update, authored at 960p, hardzao's resolution; CS2 rescales it to your resolution. Many pros rebuilt their crosshair after the update, so this may not be what hardzao uses now — see their crosshair history.

Valve published no conversion formula. This uses a community reconstruction (small-indie-crosshair-company v0.19.1, model community-static-v11, CS2 build 2000922) that hasn't been checked against in-game captures.

Download .cfg

The CS2 console can reject a pasted line of 510 characters or more, so the full config comes in parts: press ~ in CS2, paste part 1, press Enter, then do the same with the next part. Or download the .cfg, save it in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/ and run exec hardzao in the console.
